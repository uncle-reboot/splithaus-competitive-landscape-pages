OurSharedPlace

Closest operational threat: a documented round-robin booking queue plus a broad, fast-shipping property hub. SplitHaus can win only if its preference-aware allocation is simpler and proven.261

Targets families and co-owners of an existing home; also supports external guest and rental workflows. The same 3–8-household steward could plausibly buy it.21

Public claims are not product tests. “Better” below is a testable hypothesis where evidence is incomplete. Ad-library searches cannot establish spend or prove no ads. SplitHaus billing/claims conflicts remain unresolved.

Where the products differ

Different

OurSharedPlace runs ordered turns, quotas and carryover. SplitHaus claims a group-wide Match using shares and date preferences; these workflows have not been tested head-to-head.21

Potential edge

SplitHaus could handle flexible versus inflexible household preferences without a live pick queue. That is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated superior outcome.1272

Rival leads

Its iOS app, public Android beta, booking approvals, guest access, documents and finances create a wider daily-use surface.27

Unproven

No shared-household trial establishes allocation quality, setup time, retention or booking integrity for either product. SplitHaus billing and reliability caveats remain.1272

What SplitHaus should match

Match the buying and scheduling job first. Do not copy an ownership-management stack without customer evidence.

  1. P0Run the same peak-season case through Match and its round-robin queue, including a skipped turn, a trade and a disputed edit.1272 Proof: Organizer can explain outcomes; no lost or double bookings.
  2. P1Match a clear household invite/approval and reminder path before copying its blog, finances or rental modules.21 Proof: All households can see and act on one schedule.
  3. P1Make mobile booking and calendar-sharing reliable; validate demand before committing to two native apps or rental sync.721 Proof: Field users complete a booking/change on their phones.
  4. P2Reconcile the public trial/price and finance-add-on scope before a price comparison or broad parity claim.1725 Proof: Checkout and copy match the approved offer.

Evidence limits

Vendor features, price and release dates are public claims. Android is a public beta, not proof of mature use. No competitor trial, household interviews, traffic analytics or spend ledger was accessed. SplitHaus source-level behavior and billing are not reconciled; potential advantages need a controlled test.27
