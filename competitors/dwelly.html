Dwelly

A fast-shipping shared-home operations product for the same family buyer, but its published scheduling stops at booking and conflict prevention. SplitHaus needs to prove a fairness outcome, not match every household checklist.56571

Originated with three families sharing a lake house; also markets to solo second-home owners, broadening beyond SplitHaus’s multi-household beachhead.5856

Public claims are not product tests. “Better” below is a testable hypothesis where evidence is incomplete. Ad-library searches cannot establish spend or prove no ads. SplitHaus billing/claims conflicts remain unresolved.

Where the products differ

Different

Dwelly’s room and whole-house bookings prevent overlaps; SplitHaus claims a policy that decides scarce peak dates before a booking.56571

Potential edge

If the family has recurring peak-week disputes, a defensible preference/priority outcome could matter more than Dwelly’s grocery inventory. This remains untested.15672

Rival leads

Published releases through August add meal planning, viewer roles, recurring property events and .ics booking emails; SplitHaus has not demonstrated equivalent breadth.571

Unproven

Dwelly’s no-conflict claim is vendor copy; SplitHaus reliability and household adoption are also unproven.56172

What SplitHaus should match

Match the buying and scheduling job first. Do not copy an ownership-management stack without customer evidence.

  1. P0Pass DB-backed overlapping-booking and changed-rule tests before contrasting a claimed fairness engine with Dwelly’s conflict prevention.15672 Proof: No double bookings, lost changes or rule mismatch.
  2. P1Enable steward booking for relatives and a clear read-only calendar path before copying inventory and meal features.157 Proof: A non-technical household member can follow the season.
  3. P1Offer calendar export or notifications only after observing where families still fall back to chat.571 Proof: Member uses a real booking update without manual relay.
  4. P2Borrow its dated release-note transparency, not its entire house-ops roadmap.5772 Proof: Release notes map to tested scheduling improvements.

Evidence limits

Latest directly checked changelog entry is August 17, 2026, not May 30. Native app-store releases are not verified; the site says mobile web and planned apps. Founder customer remarks are unverified vendor claims. Dwelly here is dwellyco.com, not the funded UK lettings namesake.5756
