A low-cost shared-cottage hub active by 2011, with booking and finances but no documented seasonal-allocation engine. Its trust history is harder to match than its features.734446

Built for a multibranch family cottage in Canada and sold across North America; a U.S. family steward could use it for routine booking and house communication.4544

Where the products differ

Different

SharedKey centers the calendar, guest information and family finances. SplitHaus claims to execute the family’s seasonal allocation policy.441

Potential edge

Match could remove the steward’s peak-week negotiation rather than only display available dates. No comparison has tested that outcome.14472

Rival leads

A site active by 2011, $49 annual price and native store listings set a low-friction baseline.734652

Unproven

No share-weighted peak draft appears in checked public pages; absence in copy does not prove absence in the app. Neither retention nor fairness is independently measured.44451

What SplitHaus should match

Match the buying and scheduling job first. Do not copy an ownership-management stack without customer evidence.

  1. P0Demonstrate how SplitHaus handles a real contested holiday rather than simply offering another color-coded calendar.14472 Proof: Steward reaches an explainable outcome without manual arbitration.
  2. P1Make guest visibility, family invitations and a simple house-information path work for less-technical relatives.441 Proof: All household roles can read the schedule and join.
  3. P1Decide whether expense allocation belongs in core or a validated add-on before claiming finance parity.4472 Proof: Live billing and balances match approved copy.
  4. P2Seek dated customer proof and referrals instead of trying to manufacture a long operating history.455072 Proof: Permissioned household stories support a trust claim.

Evidence limits

SharedKey’s calendar and finance claims are from its site, not an account test. The attempted LinkedIn company URL returned 404 during this review; that does not prove all social channels stopped. Its 2011 archive establishes earlier site activity, not the present company’s exact founding date.7344
