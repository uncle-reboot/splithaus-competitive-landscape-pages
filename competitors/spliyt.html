Spliyt

The closest stated share-based allocation engine, but a much broader and pricier co-ownership stack. Its fairness, customer usage and paid acquisition are not independently verified.41

Housing offer targets existing jointly owned homes, typically 2–8 co-owners; the same vendor also sells tools for other assets and formal ownership governance.427

Public claims are not product tests. “Better” below is a testable hypothesis where evidence is incomplete. Ad-library searches cannot establish spend or prove no ads. SplitHaus billing/claims conflicts remain unresolved.

Where the products differ

Different

Spliyt claims share-based quotas, automated rule enforcement, e-sign, voting and maintenance. SplitHaus positions itself around family agreement-to-schedule, not legal or governance operations.41

Potential edge

A family-specific setup and group-wide preference allocation might be easier than configuring a full co-ownership stack. There is no comparative product test.1472

Rival leads

Its public offer claims ownership-share quotas, voting, settlements, document signatures and vendor dispatch under one plan. These are claims, not verified shipping.4

Unproven

The AI scheduling and “no disputes” promises have no independent household outcome evidence. SplitHaus Match and booking integrity are also unproven.4172

What SplitHaus should match

Match the buying and scheduling job first. Do not copy an ownership-management stack without customer evidence.

  1. P0Prove a complete share-weighted peak allocation against Spliyt’s stated quota and rotation policy, with a disputed holiday.1472 Proof: Explainable output survives edits and concurrent requests.
  2. P1Present the family decision flow without copying legal, signature or ownership-transfer modules by default.14 Proof: A steward can win group approval with one clear example.
  3. P1Show share and preference inputs, auditability and changed-plan handling in a no-card preview.14 Proof: Every household understands why it received dates.
  4. P2Resolve the billing and finance-scope conflict before invoking the large list-price gap in external copy.1724 Proof: Approved checkout and comparison use identical terms.

Evidence limits

The co-ownership rule engine, AI assistant, customer stories and trial terms are vendor-published claims; no product account or payment flow was tested. A visible Instagram profile does not establish launch or founding dates. Do not confuse Spliyt with namesake Splyt.429
