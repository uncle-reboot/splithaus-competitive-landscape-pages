The Vacation Calendar

A low-cost calendar/house-content substitute with a site active by 2008, not a documented automated peak allocator. It is the clearest confirmed Google Ads advertiser in this set, but recent activity is not established.743640

Markets private shared-home scheduling with rooms, bulletin board and guest content; a budget-minded family may choose it instead of a policy engine.3536

Public claims are not product tests. “Better” below is a testable hypothesis where evidence is incomplete. Ad-library searches cannot establish spend or prove no ads. SplitHaus billing/claims conflicts remain unresolved.

Where the products differ

Different

Its visible product is member-managed stays and optional approvals, not a documented group-wide share or preference allocation. SplitHaus claims that policy step.35371

Potential edge

SplitHaus can justify a premium if Match produces a better peak-season outcome, not merely a prettier calendar. The benefit is untested.13672

Rival leads

iOS and Android store listings, room booking, nine extra properties on Premium, plus a cheap yearly plan.363738

Unproven

Neither a public customer count nor a household peak-conflict outcome was verified. Historic ads do not establish current spend or acquisition efficiency.3542

What SplitHaus should match

Match the buying and scheduling job first. Do not copy an ownership-management stack without customer evidence.

  1. P0Reliably handle self-service booking, approvals and a disputed peak-week allocation before selling algorithmic advantage.13772 Proof: No overlaps or lost bookings in DB-backed tests.
  2. P1Let one steward demonstrate why Match beats a $40–$80/year calendar for their actual holiday conflict.136 Proof: Buyer names the saved coordination step.
  3. P1Make mobile group invitations and booking unambiguous; do not build photos/guestbook unless the same buyer asks.361 Proof: Less-technical household member completes the flow.
  4. P2Test search ads only after activation and billing governance are verified; historic rival creatives are a channel clue, not a budget prescription.4072 Proof: Qualified steward activation, not clicks alone.
