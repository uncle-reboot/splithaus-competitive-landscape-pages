Executive finding

SplitHaus should not position itself as the only shared-house calendar or the only fairness tool.61910 OurSharedPlace publishes a round-robin booking queue, and Shared Holiday Homes describes seasonal rotations.619 The Vacation Calendar documents booking rules and approvals, while Spliyt markets share-weighted peak-period rotation.1013 The defendable hypothesis is narrower: a simpler way for an existing multibranch family to encode its own allocation policy, incorporate preferences and shares, run a season, and handle changed plans without turning the home into a property-management project. SplitHaus's public site describes its preference-and-share-based Match feature, but runtime effectiveness and comparative superiority have not been tested.1610

Most relevant head-to-head tests: OurSharedPlace, Shared Holiday Homes, The Vacation Calendar, CabinPals, SharedKey, Ripazo and Spliyt. Price pressure: SharedKey publicly lists $49/property/year, Family Vacation Home $24.99/year with a five-member cap, and Cazmate offers the first home free.93225 SplitHaus's public page displays $119/house/year after a one-year free period, but its internal billing/claims record has an unresolved discrepancy.15 Do not reuse the SplitHaus price as approved external copy or infer willingness to pay from competitors' list prices.93225

1. SplitHaus baseline and comparison rules

SplitHaus's internal baseline identifies Matt Wells and a co-founder identified as Dan; Dan's surname is not established in the reviewed record.5 The intended buyer/use case is an existing, co-owned or co-used multigenerational family home, especially a 3–8-household seasonal scheduling problem with a calendar steward.5 The internal product record describes rotation, open booking, draft, manual allocation, grouped allocations, invitations, trades/cancellations/released dates, and conflict checks at source level; it expressly does not establish production reliability.5 The current public page markets a shared calendar, turn-taking or draft, preference-and-share Match, member reminders, changed-date handling, and an expenses proposition.1 The public page displays $119 per house/year after one year free with the group included.1 The older internal claims ledger and later founder offer have not been reconciled, and finance positioning conflicts with an earlier scheduling-only scope decision.5 Treat all price/finance/reliability claims as internal comparison inputs requiring founder/billing review before publication.512

Compare the job “agree and execute our household's use of an already shared home”, not the generic phrase “vacation ownership.” A fractional-purchase marketplace helps someone acquire a new share and then manages that company's homes.39 A rental PMS manages guests and inventory, while a generic calendar displays dates but does not, on the reviewed help page, describe family-branch allocation policy.1551 Those offerings can still win the same attention, budget, or search query, but their models differ.391551

4. The actual substitute: a no-new-app operating stack

A family can keep a shared Google Calendar with edit permissions, a Google Sheet for scheduling/dues, and Splitwise for expense reconciliation, with group text/email for decisions.515253 Cozi offers a general family calendar ($39/year Gold, $79/year Max, each covering the family).54 Notion and Airtable offer flexible templates and collaboration but charge for paid tiers by seat, not by vacation property.5560 These products document pieces of the workflow, not a ready-made family-branch allocation policy.515460 That is a scope observation, not proof families find the stack inadequate.515253

Splitwise Shared expenses, with Pro receipt scanning; no property-allocation mechanism documented in the reviewed Pro page.53 Don't sell expense capture alone against an incumbent people already use.

Other complements are lawyers for title/agreements, local home-watch and maintenance vendors, and rental PMS for groups who operate a rental business. SplitHaus should route those needs to appropriate providers rather than imply it is trust administration, legal advice, a rental manager, or an investment vehicle.52

5. Competitive implications and recommended experiments

01

Do not claim “only fair calendar.” Publicly documented round-robin, peak rotations and share-weighted scheduling exist.61913

Position on a particular existing family's policy implementation and seasonal decision, and demonstrate it with the same 3–8-household/peak-date scenario in every competitive test.61913

02

Differentiate policy fit, not feature count. In an opt-in moderated comparison, give each product the same household shares, last-year priority, unavailable dates, peak holidays, one late cancellation, one no-email relative and one disputed edit. Record whether an ordinary organizer can enter the actual rule, explain a result, revise it, and export/communicate the decision. Treat unsupported rules as “not found in reviewed flow,” not “impossible.” This is a proposed test, not a completed test.51

03

Treat adoption and trust as the gate. The internal baseline says the steward must persuade a family decision group and get households participating; a low price or broad feature list does not validate activation.5 Test steward setup, second-household acceptance, first real season allocation, member-initiated change, and continued use next season. Do not assert conversion/retention numbers from public competitor pages.54

04

Protect reliability before a comparative fairness claim. Internal source-level QA has unresolved booking-integrity and rule-mismatch risks and no DB-backed test result.5 A claim that SplitHaus is more trustworthy or objectively fair would outrun the evidence. Reconcile billing/free-trial language and decide whether expenses is core, optional or a separate category before using a published feature/price matrix in marketing.51

05

Use price as a design constraint, not WTP proof. The verified public set spans free tiers and $24.99/year with a five-member cap.2532

Other public offers include $49/property/year unlimited, $99/property/year unlimited, and $149/home/month for a more expansive product.9713 These are offers, not verified sales, comparable implementations, support costs or willingness to pay. Show the exact plan unit, limits, trial/card requirement, geography and source date on every update.25329

Ongoing competitive-analysis operating method

Keep three lanes: direct existing-home software, manual/horizontal stack, and ownership-purchase/management. Identify a fourth lane only if a customer's switching decision actually includes it. The SBA recommends examining both competing products/services and market segments, demand, saturation, and pricing; desk research and direct customer research answer different questions.4

Maintain a living ledger per vendor: canonical domain; founder name and provenance; target home and payer; booking/fairness rule; roles, expenses, tasks/docs, trial/card, price/currency/unit/limits; evidence URL, page date if visible, access date; whether verified in a no-login walkthrough; confidence and next check. For this snapshot, page publication/modified dates were generally not stated on the reviewed pages; the access date is this report's as-of date, not a content-update date.

On an agreed cadence, refresh primary pages and separately run a permitted no-login product walk-through if useful. Observe real tasks rather than copying landing-page adjectives. Track claim, observed behavior, and customer-perceived value in separate columns. Competitor feature lists do not establish reliability or outcomes.45

If the founders want actual competitive win/loss evidence, recruit only consenting users through an explicitly authorized channel and ask what they currently use, who approves the purchase, what annual scheduling event matters, which rules cannot be lost, and what would make changing tools unacceptable. This review did not recruit anyone and cannot validate segment prevalence, buyer authority, willingness to pay or switching conversion.54

Unresolved facts: Dan's full name; founders of Spliyt, The Vacation Calendar, Family Vacation Home and Fraxioned; legal founder identity for Dwelly and Shared Holiday Homes; current checkout prices; live behavior of each allocation engine; SplitHaus production booking integrity, live billing terms, and whether its finance proposition is in approved scope.528 Unknown is not absent.
